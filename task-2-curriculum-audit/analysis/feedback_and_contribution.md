## Limitations

1. The registered programme title, admission batch and curriculum version have not been confirmed from an official student-specific record.
2. The portal screenshots list the courses and credits but do not include the detailed syllabus, course outcomes, lab manuals, assessment rubrics or full regulations.
3. The official public IFHE programme structure has courses/codes/semester allocations that do not fully match the supplied screenshots; it is not used as a substitute for batch-specific records.
4. The Semester VIII internship value (15 credits) was reported by the student; no official document or screenshot confirming it was supplied.
5. Job evidence is a purposive sample of three dated postings, not a representative labour-market study. Two postings have explicit prior experience requirements; DXC is titled Junior but does not state years.
6. The job descriptions and course titles can support proposed outcomes to assess, but cannot prove that the current syllabus lacks those outcomes.
7. No stakeholder feedback or approval of proposed credit movements has been obtained.
8. Credit reductions are conditional design assumptions; if regulations do not permit them, integrate the proposed activities inside existing credits instead.
9. The user's local Python run confirms the 12 scripted checks only; it does not constitute a complete code review or validation of all possible inputs.
10. Browser interaction has been checked through the displayed course explorer output, but automated end-to-end browser tests have not been run.
