## Limitations of the job sample
The three dated postings are a small convenience sample; Wipro and Amgen explicitly require prior experience, while DXC is titled Junior but states no years of experience. The supplemental fresher listing is useful but its publication date was not confirmed. Requirements in job advertisements are signals to compare with the curriculum—not proof that every technology is compulsory for every graduate role. A stronger study would add more dated entry-level roles from several employers and record a consistent selection method.
